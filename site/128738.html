How it works

Nothing here works alone.

i

Preorders open the shelf early

We set up your preorder listing correctly and start driving traffic to it weeks before launch — so your book opens with orders already stacked, not from zero.

ii

Video finds the readers

Genre-matched short-form content goes live on TikTok, Shorts, and Reels. Readers who don't know you yet start seeing your book where they spend time.

iii

Reviews make the click worthwhile

ARC readers from our network leave honest early reviews. By the time your campaigns go live, your listing already looks credible.

iv

The listing earns the sale

Rewritten copy and an A+ page do the selling. Traffic that lands on your listing converts because the page was built to convert.

v

Launch day lands with a stacked rank

Every preorder converts to a day-one sale at once, giving Amazon's algorithm a concentrated signal that pushes your category rank hard on release day.

vi

Free promos move the rank

A newsletter feature during your KDP free days drives a download spike. That rank carries into paid sales when the price goes back up.

vii

Reader lists pay off forever

ShelfMates and StoryPals build an audience you own. Every new release reaches readers who've already said yes to you — at no extra cost.

viii

Campaigns bring them back to buy

Amazon campaigns retarget readers your content already warmed up. They click. They buy. The data feeds back in and the whole system gets sharper.

Chapter Five

Don't launch to an empty room.

Most authors open their book to no orders and hope the algorithm notices. We set up your preorder listing the right way — pricing, release-date timing, and back-of-book links all correct from day one — then run a dedicated campaign in the weeks before release that turns your video audience, ARC readers, and reader-list subscribers into locked-in preorders. When release day arrives, every one of those preorders converts at once, giving Amazon's ranking algorithm the concentrated signal it rewards most.

weeks of preorder campaign runway built into Momentum and above
  • Preorder listing setup — pricing, release date, and metadata checked before it goes live
  • A dedicated countdown content series across your video channels
  • ShelfMates / StoryPals subscribers notified as soon as preorders open
  • ARC readers prompted to preorder ahead of their review going live
  • All preorders land as same-day sales, concentrating your launch-day rank spike
Reader apps

A reader list that's yours, not the algorithm's.

Algorithms change. Our apps build a reader list that's yours — genre-matched people who've opted in to hear about books like yours, ready for every release you ever make, and first in line the moment a preorder opens.

Adult fiction

ShelfMates

Romance, thriller, fantasy, sci-fi — readers tag the genres they follow and get matched to new releases. Your book lands directly in the hands of people already looking for it, preorder link included.

Children's books

StoryPals

Parents browsing for their kids' next read, sorted by age range and reading level. Your picture book or early reader shows up at exactly the right moment for the right family — preorder or otherwise.
