About the Campaign

Dark fantasy with eldritch intrigue, shifting factions, and morally complex characters — A Pawn in a Dire Game sits squarely in the #DarkFantasyTok and #SwordAndSorcery niches that consistently drive organic discovery on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Reviewers compare it to The Witcher, Mistborn, and The Black Company — three of the most-cited touchstones in BookTok's dark fantasy community. The four-POV structure and Lady Alexis DeBolvier as a villain give us cinematic hooks that stop the scroll.
