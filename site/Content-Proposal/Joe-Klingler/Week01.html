Campaign Overview

Week 1 introduces the world of Mash Up through short-form thriller storytelling designed for TikTok and YouTube Shorts. The campaign blends cybercrime tension, dark internet paranoia, detective mystery, music culture, and modern surveillance aesthetics into highly bingeable vertical video hooks.
