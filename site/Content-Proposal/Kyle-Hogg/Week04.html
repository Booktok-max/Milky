Faith Comes From the Word Trust
"Fides. Trust. That single word changes everything."

Voiceover Script

The Latin word for faith is fides. It means trust. Not certainty. Not the absence of doubt. Trust. And trust, unlike proof, is relational. You don't trust a theorem. You trust a person. Kyle Hogg's entire guide to finding faith is built on this: that coming to belief in God is not like solving an equation. It is like learning to trust someone who has never once let you down.

YouTube Description

Faith means trust — not certainty. Kyle Hogg unpacks the Latin root fides to show that genuine faith is relational, not mathematical. It's not about having all the answers.
