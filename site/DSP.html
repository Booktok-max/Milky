Amazon DSP — Deeper

What makes DSP
different

DSP isn't a keyword search platform. It's a programmatic audience platform powered by Amazon's own shopping, browsing, and streaming data — with reach no other ad platform can match.

🔬

First-Party Audience Data

DSP runs on Amazon's own shopping history, browsing behaviour, and streaming habits — not third-party cookies. Your ads reach real readers based on what they actually buy and read, not inferred guesses.

🎯

Warm Retargeting Only

We use DSP exclusively to retarget people who already engaged with your organic content. Cold ad spend wastes budget on strangers. Warm retargeting reaches readers already partway to a purchase — at a fraction of the cost.

📺

Premium Amazon Inventory

Your ads appear across Prime Video, Twitch, Thursday Night Football, Amazon.com, Fire TV, Kindle, Alexa, and Amazon Fresh — plus thousands of premium third-party sites. Your readers are already on these surfaces.

🤖

AI-Powered Optimisation

Amazon's machine learning automatically optimises bids, placements, and targeting in real time — maximising ROI without requiring daily manual management from you or us.

📊

Book-Specific Metrics

Beyond click rates, DSP reports on detail page view rate (DPVR), new-to-brand readers (NTB), add-to-list (ATL) counts, and Kindle sample downloads — metrics that actually matter for book sales.

🔒

Privacy-First Architecture

DSP operates without third-party cookies using Amazon's Ad Relevance AI. Campaigns remain effective and future-proof as digital privacy standards evolve — no reliance on signals that may disappear.
