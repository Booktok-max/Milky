YouTube requires channel verification (ID verification) before external links can be posted in video descriptions. This is a standard YouTube safety measure to prevent spam.

Practical solution: Create a YouTube channel (takes only 2-3 minutes). Here's how:

  1. Sign in to YouTube with your Google account
  2. Click on your profile picture → "Create a channel"
  3. Choose "Use a custom name" and enter your author name (e.g., "J.C. Brandt Books")
  4. Upload a profile picture and banner (we can help with these)
  5. Once your channel is created, go to YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility
  6. Complete the ID verification process (usually takes 24-48 hours for approval)
  7. After verification, external links will work in your video descriptions

Final step: Add us as a manager to your channel so we can help with uploads and descriptions. Invite powerpages30@gmail.com as an Editor (YouTube Studio → Settings → Permissions → Invite). We'll handle the rest for you!
