Editorial

Make the book easier to understand, choose, and remember.

Editorial support looks at how your book is presented to readers: its promise, positioning, description, cover, metadata, and the story told around it.

Start an editorial conversation

It is not a manuscript edit or a promise of a review. It is reader-facing guidance for authors who want the book to make sense quickly to the people most likely to value it.

The work before the work

A campaign is stronger when the book has a clear point of view.

We review how the book appears in the places readers make decisions: the retailer listing, cover and thumbnail, description, categories, keywords, author positioning, and campaign language.

Then we explain what is working, what is unclear, and what could be improved. The result is a sharper presentation that gives every later campaign asset a stronger place to begin.

What we focus on

Clarity, context, and a presentation that earns the click.

Editorial support is a practical review of how the book meets a reader—not a formula applied to every genre or author.

01 — Position

Find the sharpest way in.

Clarify the reader, genre signals, and emotional promise that make a book easier to discover and remember.

02 — Presentation

Improve what the reader sees.

Identify improvements to the cover presentation, title and subtitle, description, categories, keywords, and retailer listing.

03 — Continuity

Carry the idea through.

Make sure the language around the book feels like it belongs to the work across the listing, trailer, ads, reader outreach, and launch.

How it starts
