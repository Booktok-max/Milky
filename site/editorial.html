Editorial

Make it clear what your book is, and who should read it.

Editorial support looks at how your book is presented to readers: its promise, positioning, description, cover, metadata, and the story told around it.

Start an editorial conversation

It is not a manuscript edit or a promise of a review. It is reader-facing guidance for authors who want the book to make sense quickly to the people most likely to value it.

Before we promote the book

We first make sure readers can tell what the book is and why it is for them.

Before we create ads, videos, or reader outreach, we review the places where people decide whether to keep reading: the cover, retailer listing, description, categories, keywords, author positioning, and campaign language.

We show you what is clear, what is confusing, and what is worth changing. That gives every later campaign asset a clear message to build from.

What we review

We make the reader-facing parts of the book easier to understand.

Every recommendation is tied to how a real reader finds, judges, and chooses a book.

01 — Audience

Who is this book for?

Clarify the genre, reader, and promise so the right people can recognise the book quickly.

02 — Listing

What will readers see?

Review the cover, title, subtitle, description, categories, keywords, and retailer page for gaps or mixed messages.

03 — Campaign

Does the message stay consistent?

Carry the same clear idea into videos, ads, reader outreach, and launch materials.

How it works

Three practical steps.

STEP 01

Send us the book.

Share the book link, catalogue, release timing, and the readers you want to reach.

STEP 02

We review the presentation.

We look at the listing, cover, metadata, description, positioning, and campaign language as a reader would.

STEP 03

We recommend the next changes.

You receive clear priorities and can decide whether you need editorial guidance, listing optimization, campaign content, or a combination.

+

Editorial and listing optimization do different jobs.

Editorial decides what the book needs to communicate and who it needs to reach. Listing optimization improves the words and presentation on the retailer page. Starting with the message makes the optimization more useful.
