Amazon's ranking algorithm rewards concentrated sales signals. A book that opens with zero orders and trickles in sales over weeks ranks worse than one where 100+ preorders convert on a single launch day. We spend the 6–8 weeks before release turning your video audience, ARC readers, and reader-list subscribers into locked-in preorders so launch day lands with a spike, not a whisper.
