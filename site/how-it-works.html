How it works

One system. Every stage supports the next.

A good book is only the beginning. Readers have to discover it, trust it, understand why it's for them, and make the purchase — and ideally, remember you when the next book arrives. That's why we don't treat marketing as a collection of disconnected tasks.

How Atomic Shelf works

Discover → Trust → Convert → Retain → Repeat

01

Discover

Video finds the readers.

What we do

Short-form content — TikTok, Shorts, Reels — puts your book in front of genre-relevant audiences, at every plan level. Enhanced and up add a cinematic trailer; Starter layers managed Amazon DSP spend on top.

What changes for the reader

Someone who has never heard of your book sees it for the first time, matched to their genre rather than shown at random.

02

Trust

Reviews make the click worthwhile.

What we do

Listing copy and A+ content (Foundation, Momentum, and Growth) make the storefront credible and clear. ARC campaigns (Momentum and Growth) recruit genre-matched readers for a complimentary copy, inviting an honest, independent review — never conditional on a positive rating.

What changes for the reader

A curious reader meets a clear listing and, where included, independent social proof instead of a blank or confusing sales page.

03

Convert

The listing earns the sale.

What we do

Preorder setup (Foundation and Momentum) establishes the launch infrastructure. Momentum and Growth add a structured preorder countdown and segmented retargeting to bring interested readers back to finish the purchase.

What changes for the reader

An interested reader has a clear reason and opportunity to act — before launch through a countdown, or after engagement through a relevant return visit.

04

Retain

Reader lists make the relationship repeatable.

What we do

ShelfMates and StoryPals enroll readers into a list you own (Foundation, Momentum, and Growth), not one you rent from a platform. Momentum and Growth add full social account management and scheduled newsletter features.

What changes for the reader

A one-time buyer becomes part of an audience you can reach directly — not someone you have to rediscover from zero next time.

05

Repeat

The next book starts with readers you already earned.

What we do

The same system runs again for the next release — video, trust, conversion, and retention — except this time it starts from an existing list instead of zero.

What changes for the reader

Discovering your next book stops being a coincidence and becomes something they were already set up to hear about.

What we promise, honestly

What we control. What we influence. What no one can honestly guarantee.

This distinction is meant to build credibility, not weaken the offer — anyone who promises otherwise is promising something they can't control.

What we control

  • Agreed production
  • Agreed campaigns
  • Agreed reviews / recruitment work
  • Listing optimisation
  • Reporting
  • Communication
  • Testing and iteration
  • Campaign management
  • Delivery of included services

What we influence

  • Reach
  • Engagement
  • Clicks
  • Audience growth
  • Conversion
  • Sales
  • Reviews
  • Reader acquisition
  • Repeat readership

What we cannot honestly guarantee

  • An algorithmic viral event
  • A specific number of sales regardless of market conditions
  • A bestseller position
  • A particular category ranking
  • A particular advertising platform's behaviour
  • Reader behaviour

This is also exactly what the Atomic Shelf Commitment below covers — and doesn't.

The first month establishes the foundation. The following months give the work enough time to learn, compound, and improve.

Our commitment

We don't promise that every book will explode overnight. Publishing doesn't work that way — and neither does building a readership.

What we do promise is that we'll do the work we've committed to, measure what happens, and stay accountable for the outcome.

If we fall short of the work, delivery, or agreed commitments on our side, we'll make it right — at our cost.

And when the numbers show that we need to change course, we don't simply point at the dashboard and walk away. We diagnose, adjust, and keep working toward the target.

We commit to doing the work. You give it the time and access it needs to work.

Results

What to expect from the work.

Results build over the 90-day cycle: the first 30 days establish the foundation and baseline metrics, days 31–60 build momentum and strengthen social proof, and days 61–90 measure against baseline and identify what's working. Meaningful, sustained movement is more realistic by month 3 than month 1.

We establish positioning, prepare content and creative assets, begin discovery-stage posting, and set the baseline metrics everything else gets measured against.

Short answer: No.

Long answer: Although it is possible to estimate expected downloads or sales for lower-priced books, those outcomes cannot be guaranteed because they depend on reader behaviour, market conditions, and platform performance. We control the work we commit to — production, campaigns, reviews recruitment, listing optimisation, reporting. We influence reach, clicks, and conversion. We don't guarantee a specific number of sales, a bestseller position, a ranking, or an algorithmic viral event — no one honestly can.

Depends on your plan's stage: content-only plans report impressions, video views, click-throughs, and sales lift; campaign plans (Momentum/Growth) also report preorders secured, free promo downloads, and KU page-reads boost.
