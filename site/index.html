What we do

Five levers. One system.

Most authors run these separately, from different vendors, with no shared data. We run all five as a single loop — so each stage makes the next one cheaper and more effective.

01

Short-form video production

60+ genre-calibrated videos per month — scripts drawn from your book's reader psychology, produced at AI-assisted pace, posted to TikTok, Shorts, and Reels. Higher tiers add book trailers, long-form YouTube, and custom theme songs.

ARC review campaigns

03

Listing copy & A+ optimisation

Why it works

The system that gets stronger over time.

When the same team runs video, reviews, listing, and retargeting off the same data, each stage amplifies the next. The longer it runs, the more momentum it builds.

Video → Impressions

Builds the warm audience

Genre-calibrated content reaches cold readers organically. Every view is a trackable signal — who watched, how long, what they clicked.

Reviews → Trust

Gives the listing credibility

Strangers don't buy from blank listings. ARC reviews give cold traffic a reason to stop scrolling before they've heard of you.

Listing → Conversion

Turns traffic into sales

Optimised copy and A+ content mean the readers your content and reviews warm up actually complete the purchase.

Retargeting → Intent

Closes the loop

Warm viewers get shown the book again — and again — until passive awareness tips into a purchase. Every sale feeds better data back into the next campaign.
