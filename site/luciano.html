You've crossed the border before. So why does it still feel like a gamble?

One wrong answer. One undeclared item. One officer having a bad day — and suddenly you're waved into secondary inspection, your car is being searched, and you have no idea what your rights are or how long you'll be there.

Crossing the Line gives you the insider knowledge CBSA officers won't volunteer.

Luciano Nisi walks you through exactly what happens from the moment you pull up to the booth to the moment you're released. Not theory. Not government pamphlets. Practical, honest answers to the questions travelers actually have.

You'll learn:

  • What ID you actually need — and what you can leave at home
  • What you can and can't buy Duty Free, and why the rules catch people off guard
  • Why CBSA sends some travelers inside and waves others through
  • What "random exam" actually means and what to expect
  • How to handle duty calculations and declarations without panic
  • What happens if you don't disclose something — and when that becomes serious

Whether you cross for work, shopping, family, or travel — this book removes the guesswork.

Cross with confidence. Know your rights. Never feel caught off guard at the booth again.
