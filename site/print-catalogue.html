The System

Every stage feeds the next one

Five stages, run as one loop. Each one makes the next cheaper, warmer, and more likely to end in a sale.

i

Content deploys

Genre-calibrated video goes live across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Reels, optimized for discovery in your exact niche.

ii

Reviews land

ARC readers leave the early, honest reviews that give your listing credibility with strangers who've never heard of you.

iii

The listing converts

Optimized copy, A+ banners, and social proof turn the traffic content and reviews send into completed purchases.

iv

Reader lists compound

ShelfMates and StoryPals grow an owned audience you can reach again at zero marginal ad cost on every future release.

v

Advertising closes the sale

Managed campaigns retarget warm readers right when purchase intent peaks, and every sale feeds attribution data back into the loop.
