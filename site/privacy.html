Atomic Shelf takes your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our website and services, why we collect it, how we use it, and what rights you have. We aim to be clear and direct — if anything here is unclear, please reach out to nick@atomic-shelf.com.

1

Who We Are

Atomic Shelf (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website at atomic-shelf.com and provides AI-powered book marketing services to independent authors. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, Atomic Shelf is the data controller of personal data collected through this website and our services.

You can contact us at nick@atomic-shelf.com with any privacy-related queries.

Legal Basis for Processing

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract — processing necessary to deliver the Services you have subscribed to (account data, billing, content production, campaign management).
  • Legitimate interests — improving our Services, detecting fraud, security monitoring, and direct marketing to existing clients, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process data to comply with applicable law (e.g. tax records, responding to lawful requests from authorities).
  • Consent — for optional marketing communications and for placing non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. Here is what we use and why:

Type Purpose Can you opt out?

Analytics Understanding how visitors use the site (page views, session duration, referral source) Yes — via your browser settings or our cookie banner

Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Services and meet our legal obligations.

Data type Retention period
Account & billing records 7 years from the end of the subscription (tax and accounting obligations)
Campaign and performance data 3 years after the end of your subscription
Support communications 3 years from the date of the communication
Creative content submitted by you Deleted within 90 days of account closure on written request
Analytics / cookies Up to 26 months (standard analytics window)

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

International Data Transfers

Atomic Shelf operates globally and some of our service providers are based outside the UK or European Economic Area (EEA). Where we transfer personal data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the relevant authority
  • Adequacy decisions recognising the destination country's data protection standards
  • Other lawful transfer mechanisms under applicable law

You can request details of the specific safeguards in place for any transfer by contacting us at nick@atomic-shelf.com.

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. We honour all valid requests within 30 days.

Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Correction

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Erasure

Request deletion of your data where we no longer have a lawful reason to hold it.

Restriction

Ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.

Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Objection

Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing.

Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent at any time where processing is consent-based.

Automated Decisions

Not be subject to solely automated decisions with significant legal effects.

To exercise any of these rights, email nick@atomic-shelf.com with a description of your request. We may need to verify your identity before responding. There is no charge for exercising your rights unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.
