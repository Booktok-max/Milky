Your 3-Phase Growth Strategy
📊 This is the full picture of how we grow Bermuda's Triangle from zero to a consistent sales engine. Each phase builds on the last.
P1
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4
Organic Content First
We post 2–3 short-form videos per day on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. The goal is to build an audience around your book's themes — desire, loyalty, danger, forbidden love — before spending a single dollar on ads. Organic views tell the algorithm who your reader is. This data is priceless for the phases that follow.
Target: 10,000–30,000 organic views in Month 1
P2
Phase 2 · After Month 1
TikTok Promote — Lookalike Expansion
Once we have organic data, we activate TikTok Promote on your top-performing videos. Promote takes your real viewers and finds lookalike audiences — people with the same interests, behaviors, and demographics — at a fraction of traditional ad costs. At this stage, we're amplifying what's already working, not guessing.
Typical CPM at this stage: $3–6 · Starts after Month 1
P3
Phase 3 · Ongoing
Amazon DSP — Direct Retargeting to Book Page
This is the highest-leverage phase. Amazon DSP (Demand-Side Platform) lets us place display ads directly in front of people who have already seen your TikTok or YouTube content — and send them straight to your Amazon book page. DSP drives direct reader traffic to a purchase-ready page.

Without DSP, video views don't reliably convert to sales. With DSP retargeting, you close the loop — views become clicks, clicks become reads and purchases.

Because we've built a content-first audience, your DSP CPM drops dramatically: instead of the standard $10 CPM, content-warmed audiences typically cost us around $2 CPM — 80% cheaper, and far more targeted.
DSP CPM: ~$2 (vs $10 cold) · Content-first advantage
Why This Order Matters
Without Content First
Cold DSP costs $8–12 CPM. You're targeting anonymous audiences with no prior exposure. Conversion rates are low. Views don't stick. Budget burns fast.
With Content First (Our Approach)
Warm DSP retargeting costs ~$2 CPM. Audiences already know your book. They've seen the hook. DSP closes the loop and sends them directly to buy. Every dollar works harder.
The Result
Readers who find Bermuda's Triangle organically, get retargeted on Amazon, and click through to your page are far more likely to buy or read on Kindle Unlimited — giving you real sales lift, not just vanity metrics.
