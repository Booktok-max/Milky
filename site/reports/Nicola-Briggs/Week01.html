Your 3-Phase Growth Strategy
📊 This is the full picture of how we grow Peacock on the Moon from discovery to a consistent sales engine. Each phase builds on the last.
P1
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4
Organic Content First
We post 2–3 short-form videos per day on TikTok and YouTube Shorts, targeting hashtags like #MemoirTok, #ComingOfAge, #FamilySecrets, and #BookTok. The goal is to build an audience around the book's themes — identity, belonging, prejudice, a daughter finding her own voice — before spending a single dollar on ads. Organic views tell the algorithm who your reader is.
Target: 10,000–30,000 organic views in Month 1
P2
Phase 2 · After Month 1
TikTok Promote — Lookalike Expansion
Once we have organic data, we activate TikTok Promote on your top-performing videos. Promote takes your real viewers and finds lookalike audiences — people with the same interests, behaviors, and demographics — at a fraction of traditional ad costs. At this stage, we're amplifying what's already working, not guessing.
Typical CPM at this stage: $3–6 · Starts after Month 1
P3
Phase 3 · Ongoing
Amazon DSP — Direct Retargeting to Book Page
Amazon DSP lets us place display ads directly in front of people who have already seen your TikTok or YouTube content — and send them straight to your Amazon book page. Because we've built a content-first audience, your DSP CPM drops dramatically: instead of the standard $10 CPM, content-warmed audiences typically cost around $2 CPM — 80% cheaper, far more targeted.

Without DSP, video views don't reliably convert to sales. With DSP retargeting, you close the loop — views become clicks, clicks become purchases.
DSP CPM: ~$2 (vs $10 cold) · Content-first advantage
Why Memoir Works on BookTok
The Audience
BookTok readers devour emotionally honest coming-of-age stories. Memoirs about difficult families, boarding school life, and class/race dynamics generate enormous engagement — readers share them because they see themselves.
The Hashtag Opportunity
#MemoirTok, #ComingOfAge, #FamilySecrets, and #RacismStories are all active, underserved niches. A 4.4-star memoir with real depth and humour stands out immediately against the average BookTok content.
The Result
Readers who discover Peacock on the Moon through an emotionally resonant short-form clip, get retargeted on Amazon, and click through — are readers who will leave reviews, recommend to friends, and come back for your next book.
