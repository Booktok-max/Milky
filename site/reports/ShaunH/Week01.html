3-Phase Growth Plan
📊 This is the full roadmap for growing Kokoro from zero to a consistent sales engine on Amazon and Kindle Unlimited. Each phase builds on the last.
P1
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4
Organic Content First
We post 2–3 short-form videos per day on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. The goal is to build an audience around Kokoro's themes — sacrifice, unbreakable bonds, gods and fate, the will to fight for love — before spending a single dollar on ads. Organic views tell the algorithm exactly who your reader is. With anime-adjacent content, the algorithm is your best friend: #AnimeBookTok, #LightNovel, and #MartialArtsFantasy are active, hungry communities.
Target: 10,000–30,000 organic views in Month 1
P2
Phase 2 · After Month 1
TikTok Promote — Lookalike Expansion
Once we have organic data, we activate TikTok Promote on your top-performing videos. Promote takes your real viewers and finds lookalike audiences — people with matching interests, behaviors, and demographics — at a fraction of traditional ad costs. We're amplifying what's already working, not guessing cold.
Typical CPM at this stage: $3–6 · Activates after Month 1
P3
Phase 3 · Ongoing
Amazon DSP — Direct Retargeting to Book Page
Amazon DSP lets us place display ads directly in front of people who have already seen your TikTok or YouTube content — and send them straight to your Amazon listing. This is where video views become real purchases and KU reads.

Without DSP, video views don't reliably convert. With DSP retargeting on a warm audience, you close the loop. Content-warmed audiences typically cost us around $2 CPM vs the standard $10 CPM cold — 80% cheaper and far more targeted.
DSP CPM: ~$2 (vs $10 cold) · Content-first advantage
The Kokoro Advantage
Perfect Review Score
27 reviews at 5.0 stars. That's extremely rare on Amazon. We lead with this prominently — social proof this strong is a campaign asset in itself.
