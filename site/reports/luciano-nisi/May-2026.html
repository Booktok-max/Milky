The Next Step: TikTok Promote

Turning organic momentum into targeted reach
🎉
Phase One Complete — You've Built a Real Organic Audience
8,521 TikTok views in just 18 days is a strong signal. People are watching, engaging, and following without any paid amplification. That organic proof of concept is exactly what you need before putting any budget behind a video — it means you're promoting content that already works, not guessing in the dark.
What's Available Now
TikTok Promote

TikTok Promote lets you take a video that's already performing — one with real views and engagement — and push it to a much wider audience. TikTok identifies people who look and behave like your existing viewers (lookalike audiences) and serves them your content. You stay in control: you set the budget, you choose the geography, and you can pause at any time. No advertising experience required.

🎯
Lookalike Audiences
TikTok finds new viewers who match the profile of people already engaging with your content — same interests, same behaviour, same reading appetite.
🌍
You Choose the Countries
Because you're promoting directly through your own TikTok account, you pick the geographies — prioritising premium English-speaking markets like the US, UK, Canada, and Australia where book buyers are most active.
💰
Flexible Daily Budget
You can start with as little as a few dollars a day. Set a total budget cap and TikTok stops automatically when you hit it. No surprise charges, no commitment beyond what you choose.
How it works for you
1
Log into your TikTok Business account at ads.tiktok.com/promote-101 ↗ — this is TikTok's own step-by-step guide for Promote.
2
Choose one of your existing videos to promote — your best-performing TikToks are the strongest candidates to amplify first.
3
Set your goal (more video views), select your target countries, and choose a daily budget. Review TikTok's budgeting guidance at ads.tiktok.com/budgeting ↗
4
TikTok handles the rest — distributing your video to lookalike audiences and reporting results in real time. You can pause or stop at any point.

Which Videos Should You Promote First?
Your organic data gives you a clear starting point. The videos with the highest views and engagement are already proven with a cold audience — those are the ones worth amplifying.
