What we do

Nine kinds of work. One connected system.

Every service below exists to move a reader through one stage of the same system — discover the book, trust it, convert the click, retain the reader, repeat with the next release. Nothing here is sold as a disconnected add-on.

Discover

Short-form video

In every plan · trailer from Enhanced · theme song on Starter & Growth

What it is

Genre-matched short-form content — TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels — built specifically to put your book in front of readers already primed for your genre.

Why it matters

Video finds the readers. A good book is only the beginning — readers have to discover it before anything else in the system can work.

What we do

Genre-matched scripts, viral hooks, and trending audio at every tier. Enhanced and up add a cinematic book trailer; Starter and Growth add an original theme song for a consistent audio identity across the run.

What success looks like

Rising impressions and video views feeding the click-throughs later stages convert — roughly 30k–70k impressions by month 3 at Spark, scaling with plan.

Who needs it

Every author, at every stage — this is the base layer every other service in the system builds on.

Trust

ARC review campaigns

Momentum (10 readers) & Growth (25 readers)

What it is

We can make a book free, or provide complimentary copies to selected readers, then invite honest, independent reviews. The free copy is never conditional on posting a review, giving a positive rating, or changing an existing one.

Why it matters

Reviews make the click worthwhile — they create social proof and reduce the uncertainty a cold reader feels before buying from an author they don't know yet.

What we do

Recruit and manage a batch of genre-matched readers, send matched copies, and track the reviews that result.

What success looks like

Net new reviews on the target title — a measurable review-count delta before vs. after the campaign.

Who needs it

Authors moving from discovery-only content into a coordinated launch or backlist push, where a thin review count is holding back conversion.

Convert

Listing optimisation

Foundation, Starter, Momentum, Growth

What it is

Rewritten listing copy, keywords, and A+ content built to convert the traffic your video and review work is already sending.

Why it matters

The listing earns the sale. Content and reviews get a reader to click — the storefront itself has to close it.

What we do

A base copy and A+ rewrite at Foundation, an enhanced A+ build at Momentum, and a premium A+ build at Growth.

What success looks like

A higher click-through-to-listing conversion rate, isolated from any change in the traffic volume itself.

Who needs it

Authors with a live Amazon listing that's never been properly optimised — usually the first gap once discovery content is already working.

Convert

Preorder campaigns

Setup on Foundation · full countdown on Momentum & Growth

What it is

Getting the mechanical preorder infrastructure live on Amazon, then — at Momentum and Growth — running a structured countdown campaign against it.

Why it matters

A structured countdown builds momentum before launch day, turning warmed, trust-building traffic into a committed sale ahead of release rather than losing it to a generic launch date.

What we do

Foundation configures the preorder listing itself. Momentum runs a 6-week countdown campaign against it; Growth runs 8 weeks.

What success looks like

Preorders secured by launch — roughly 80–180 at Momentum, 200–420 at Growth, by month 3.

Who needs it

Authors with a launch date already on the calendar who need the run-up coordinated rather than improvised.

Discover

Free-promotion campaigns

Bundled with the newsletter feature on Momentum & Growth

What it is

A limited free-download window, pushed through the reader list and paired with a newsletter placement, to widen the reader base and lift visibility.

Why it matters

It re-engages readers already on your list while pulling in new ones at volume — a discovery lever that runs on top of, not instead of, the ongoing video work.

What we do

Run the promotion alongside a scheduled newsletter feature — quarterly at Momentum, monthly at Growth — through the shared reader list built by ShelfMates/StoryPals.

What success looks like

1,200–2,400 downloads per feature at Momentum, 2,400–4,800 at Growth, with a KU page-reads lift alongside it.

Who needs it

Authors who already have a reader list to promote into — this is a retain-stage amplifier, not a standalone discovery tool for a brand-new author.

Retain

Reader-list building

Foundation, Starter, Momentum, Growth

What it is

Converting one-time viewers or buyers into a reader list you own — via ShelfMates (adult fiction) or StoryPals (children's & family) — rather than one you rent from a platform.

Why it matters

Reader lists make the relationship repeatable. The next book starts with readers you've already earned, instead of starting discovery from zero every time.

What we do

Ongoing enrollment of new readers into ShelfMates or StoryPals, matched to your genre, as content and campaigns run.

What success looks like

Net new reader-list signups per month, and a growing list size to feed retargeting and newsletter features.

Who needs it

Any author planning more than one release — this is what makes the second book's launch faster than the first.

Convert

Audience retargeting

Momentum & Growth

What it is

Segmented and lookalike retargeting, shown up to 5x daily for 30 days to readers who already engaged with your content or listing.

Why it matters

It closes the loop — bringing an interested reader back to finish a purchase they already showed intent for, instead of losing that attention after one impression.

What we do

Run segmented retargeting against engaged visitors, plus lookalike targeting toward readers who resemble your existing converters, for 30 days per cycle.

What success looks like

Sales lift attributable to the retargeted segment — roughly +25–45% at Momentum, +60–110% at Growth by month 3.

Who needs it

Authors already running content and listing work, where the gap isn't traffic — it's finishing the sale traffic already earned.

See Momentum & Growth →
Retain

Reader ecosystem

Enrollment from Foundation · full social management on Momentum & Growth

What it is

A long-term home for your readers to follow you between releases — ShelfMates for adult fiction, StoryPals for children's and family books — plus, at Momentum and Growth, full management of your own social accounts.

Why it matters

We don't just market books. We build reader relationships. Discovery on its own fades after one campaign — a reader ecosystem is what turns it into a standing audience for the next book.

What we do

Enroll readers into ShelfMates or StoryPals as they come through the funnel, and — at Momentum and Growth — manage your own social accounts day to day so retained readers have somewhere active to follow.

What success looks like

Follower growth and engagement rate on your own accounts (Momentum/Growth), alongside a steadily growing reader-list size (Foundation and up).

Who needs it

Any author thinking beyond a single launch — the reader ecosystem is what a second and third book get to start from.
