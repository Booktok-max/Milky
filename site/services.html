Services

Everything that goes into selling one more book.

Each piece below stands on its own, but they were built to run together — content that earns trust, trust that earns a click, and a listing built to close the sale. Here's exactly what's included in each. Pricing and expected results by tier live on the pricing page.

01

Short-form video production

We write, produce, and publish genre-matched video for your book every month — no camera, no editing software, no scripts to approve line by line. Content goes out on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels, calibrated to how your specific genre's readers actually discover books.

TikTokYouTube ShortsReelsBook trailersTheme songs (higher tiers)
02

ARC & beta reader campaigns

We recruit genre-matched readers from our own network, send advance copies, brief them, and follow up until reviews land. Your listing gets honest, early social proof before it needs to convert a single stranger.

Reader recruitmentManaged follow-upGenuine reviews only
03

Preorder campaigns

We set up your preorder listing correctly — pricing, release timing, and back-of-book links — then run a countdown campaign across your video channels, ARC readers, and reader lists in the weeks before launch. Every preorder converts to a same-day sale, giving Amazon's algorithm the concentrated signal that moves category rank hardest.

Listing setup6–8 week countdown seriesLaunch-day rank spike
04

Listing & A+ page builds

Your Amazon listing gets rewritten copy and a designed A+ page — built to hold the attention your video and reviews send its way, and to turn that attention into a sale or a Kindle Unlimited borrow.

Copy rewriteA+ content buildConversion-focused design
05

Free promo & newsletter features

When your book goes free on KDP, we time a newsletter feature and a push to our ARC and beta network to coincide with it. The download spike climbs the free charts fast, and the rank momentum typically carries into paid sales for weeks after the price resets.

Newsletter placementTimed to KDP free daysAlso-bought row growth
06

Managed Amazon advertising

Once there's an audience worth advertising to, our team runs your Amazon campaigns day to day — retargeting readers who've already seen your content or reviews, right when they're closest to buying or borrowing. You see the plan and the outcomes; we run the console.

Certified Amazon Advertising PartnerWeekly optimizationFull attribution
07

Reader-list apps — ShelfMates & StoryPals

Our own in-house apps build a reader list that's yours, not a platform's. ShelfMates matches adult fiction to genre-tagged readers; StoryPals matches children's books to parents browsing by age range. Every release reaches an audience that's already said yes to you.

Owned audienceGenre & age-range matchingNo extra cost per release
