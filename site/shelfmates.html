The reader path

A better shelf starts with relevance.

ShelfMates is the adult-fiction path through the Atomic Shelf reader ecosystem. It gives readers a way to find books that fit their tastes and gives authors a more durable route into discovery.

The goal is not to turn every title into the same recommendation. It is to make the connection between a book, its genre, and the right reader easier to understand.

What it supports

Discovery that can continue after launch week.

01 - MATCH

Genre-led discovery

Help readers move from a broad interest to a title with the right emotional and genre signals.

02 - LIST

Reader continuity

Create a path for readers who want to hear about the next book, related title, or future release.

03 - CONTEXT

Useful introductions

Give each book enough context to be considered honestly, without reducing it to a generic sales claim.

For authors

ShelfMates is a reader-discovery surface, not a promise of reviews, positive ratings, or sales.

Reader lists and outreach must remain voluntary and transparent. Complimentary copies are never conditional on posting a review, giving a positive rating, or changing an existing review.
