The opportunity

Every stage of a launch can feed the next one.

  • Video builds the audience first. Readers discover your book on TikTok and Shorts before they ever see the Amazon listing — so ads have someone warm to talk to.
  • Reviews turn that audience into buyers. Genre-matched ARC readers give your listing real, early social proof, right when strangers are deciding whether to trust it.
  • And it compounds. One team running content, reviews, listing, list-building, and ads together means every launch builds on the last — shared data, one strategy, momentum that carries forward.
