The reader path

Good discovery begins with the moment the book is for.

StoryPals is the children's-book path through the Atomic Shelf reader ecosystem. It helps families discover books by age, interest, and reading occasion rather than treating every young reader as the same.

For authors, it creates a clearer way to introduce a book while keeping the decision with the parent, caregiver, educator, or reader.

What it supports

A warmer route from curiosity to a story.

01 - DISCOVER

Find the right fit

Use age, theme, format, and reading context to make a new title easier to consider.

02 - RETURN

Build continuity

Help families and educators keep track of books they may want to revisit or share next.

03 - TRUST

Keep context clear

Present children's books honestly, with enough information for an informed adult decision.

For authors

StoryPals supports discovery. It does not promise a review, rating, school placement, or sales outcome.

Reader outreach should be transparent and voluntary. A complimentary copy is never conditional on a review, positive rating, or change to an existing review.
